About Mohan Machinery Stores

A legacy of quality machinery, genuine spare parts, and reliable service in Gwalior since 1998.

Our Store Legacy

Founded in 1998 by Mr. Mohan Kumar Jain, Mohan Machinery Stores has grown to become one of Gwalior’s most dependable machinery dealers.

Located at Gust Ka Tazia Chauraha in Lashkar, we specialize in food processing machinery, flour mills, dal mill plants, electric motors, and agricultural water pumps.

We take pride in guiding our customers—from home users buying domestic chakkis to industrial clients setting up heavy pulverizer units.
